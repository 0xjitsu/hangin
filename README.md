# Hangin

**The air you're breathing now.** A free web tool that shows hourly PM2.5, PM10 and ozone for any Philippine city, now and for the next 24 hours, with plain advice for kids, elders, pregnant women and people with asthma.

**For:** parents, schools, barangay health workers, and anyone with asthma or other breathing problems.

Status: being built. See the build brief in the issues.

## Data and attribution
Air quality data comes from the [Open-Meteo Air Quality API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api) (free, no API key), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It is based on Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) data. Forecasts are model estimates, not readings from an official monitoring station, and they aren't medical advice.
